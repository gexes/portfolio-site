Project overview

Three siblings inherit AI cybernetics from their dying CEO father. Each implant is engineered to amplify their worst traits as they battle for control of a surveillance empire. Psycorp is a Unity narrative game in development. As Technical Director and Managing Director, I built the systems that connect narrative authoring, actor staging, cameras, progression, and persistent game state.

My contributions

I built Glikit, a Unity toolset connecting narrative authoring, actors, cameras, objectives, and Yarn Spinner. I also developed the menu, save-slot flow, and cross-scene restoration system.

Custom editor & narrative framework

Problem

Manual scene setup was slow and prone to missing references.

Goal

Give designers one reliable workflow for building narrative events.

Approach

Glikit creates and validates actors, flags, objectives, sequencers, camera setups, and Yarn nodes.

Outcome

Scenes are faster to build, easier to validate, and consistent across the project.

Camera pipeline & actor staging

Problem

Dialogue scenes needed reusable actor staging and reliable camera control.

Goal

Let writers direct actors and shots from Yarn without scene-specific code.

Approach

Stable actor IDs, scene configurations, and camera-anchor presets drive spawning, animation, and shot changes.

Outcome

Actors and shots can be reused while gameplay cameras restore automatically.

Yarn Spinner gameplay integration

Problem

Dialogue choices needed to change gameplay without fragile scene references.

Goal

Use Yarn as a readable control layer for narrative gameplay.

Approach

Custom commands connect Yarn to actors, cameras, objectives, flags, saves, and scene systems.

Outcome

Writers can build interactive scenes using the same state as gameplay and saves.

Main menu, save system & restoration

Problem

Saves had to rebuild a complete narrative scene without firing gameplay events.

Goal

Create a flexible multi-slot system that preserves story and gameplay state.

Approach

Stable IDs and ordered restoration rebuild global state, actors, objects, the player, and cameras.

Outcome

Players can reliably start, resume, delete, and checkpoint playthroughs.
