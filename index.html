About meA systems-minded game developer with a designer’s eye for feel, feedback, and flow.

Get to know me!

I'm Brandon Smith, a Technical Game Designer who treats gameplay systems as iterative design problems. Across 20+ projects, I've built and refined core mechanics through testing, with an emphasis on modular architecture and long-term stability.

My work bridges game design and software engineering. I build systems grounded in SOLID principles so teams can experiment, collaborate, and add features with confidence. I've led design across cross-functional teams ranging from 4 to 70+ developers.

My background includes game design studies through CUNYBA, the Thomas W. Smith Academic Fellowship, and helping newcomers explore game development at EGD Collective. Competitive play in Gears of War and Apex Legends sharpened my interest in engaging, readable mechanics.

Away from development, I enjoy cooking for friends and listening to house, disco, and lo-fi rap.
