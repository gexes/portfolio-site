Project overview

Striking Hell is a 3D platformer-action game about a cursed demon bound to a bloodthirsty sword that constantly seeks blood. Players must navigate a Hell-like world while managing the sword's uncontrollable hunger for blood, using its power both as a threat and as a tool to overcome obstacles.

Worked with the art team to integrate the environment and theme into the game

Made Level Design concepts to fit the player's mechanics and core gameplay

Gameplay & features

Fast-Paced Movement Inspired by Sonic the Hedgehog, the player runs, jumps, and navigates dynamic environments with fluid momentum.

Dual Combat Mechanics: Use stab and slash attacks as your only form of attack; stab pierces through objects/enemies, while slash glides and cuts across targets.

Resource Management: Collect blood cubes to satiate the sword's hunger, as it constantly drains the player's health, creating a sense of urgency.

Integrated Gameplay Design balancing Combat and traversal mechanics, with stabbing and slashing enabling progression through obstacles, puzzles, and enemies.

Design & development

I originally created the level with the purpose that the player would run on the rooftops, but as I used the player movement and slash & stab, it was very clear that I should be basing the mechanics on moving around from place to place with the slash and stab because they can combo and chain off of one another

Goal: Sketching levels

I wanted to make levels based on a city, and wanted to incorporate rooftop city jumping as part of the level because I thought it would be cool that you could zoom around
