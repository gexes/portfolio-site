Project overview

Project Dreamscape is a surreal 3D hack-and-slash roguelite where you shape your dreamworld and powers while battling inner demons. Expand your dream one land at a time and unleash combos with a shape-shifting hammer to survive the night. This game was developed by the Studio Aspen team of 2024-2025, a student-led AAA simulation studio from EGD Collective.

My contributions

Balanced and tuned enemy archetypes to define clear combat roles and maintain consistent Gameplay Pacing.

Adjusted Health, Damage, and Stagger thresholds to create fair but challenging encounters.

Collaborated with Design and QA to evaluate combat readability and refine player response windows.

Authored documentation outlining Tuning Rationale, data changes, and iteration goals for future balancing passes.

Gameplay & features

Execute light, heavy, and charged melee combos with a shape-shifting hammer, tuned for responsive timing and crowd control.

Unlock and equip Aspects that modify combat stats, abilities, and behavior, enabling build diversity across roguelite runs.

Navigate procedurally generated dreamworlds with branching paths, dynamic enemy compositions, and environmental hazards.

Face distinct enemy archetypes—swarmers, disruptors, and AOE controllers—designed to challenge positioning, pacing, and tactical adaptation.

Design & development

Goal: Enemy Combat Balance

I wanted to refine enemy behaviors to match the enemy's role and purpose better. I focused on establishing distinct combat roles across enemy archetypes to improve encounter readability.

Define enemy types by behavior and combat purpose—Followers for pressure, Chargers for burst disruption, and Golems for area control.

Balance time-to-kill, stagger recovery, and detection ranges to create a smooth rhythm between high-intensity and recovery moments.

Adjust visual and behavioral telegraphs to make enemy intent clear and reaction windows fair.

Tune stats to support group composition diversity, ensuring that mixed enemy setups feel emergent and tactical.

Documented data-driven parameters and balancing logic to streamline future difficulty tuning and design iteration

Balancing & Testing

Goal: Enemy Role Differentiation

My goal was to define and reinforce each enemy's identity so that every archetype served a distinct purpose within the combat loop. I focused on how behaviors, movement patterns, and stat tuning could communicate intent and create natural synergy between enemy types, encouraging players to adapt their strategy mid-fight.

Follower

Problem: Followers lacked pressure and presence in group encounters. Low health and inconsistent damage made them trivial, reducing tension and pacing. Solution: Increased health and speed to improve durability and engagement frequency. Tightened damage range to stabilize threat level and reinforce their role as persistent crowd units.

Charger

Problem: Chargers dealt unpredictable burst damage and had poor telegraphing, leading to unfair hits and limited counterplay. Their long charge duration also caused pacing issues. Solution: Reduced damage variance and shortened charge duration to improve readability. Increased contact damage and detection range to reinforce their role as reactive disruptors.

Golem

Problem: Golems were too easy to interrupt, undermining their role as spatial threats. Their AOE attacks lacked impact, and they didn't force meaningful repositioning. Solution: Boosted health, smash force, and dazed threshold to enhance their durability and disruptive potential. Now they anchor combat zones and demand strategic movement.
