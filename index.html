About meA systems-minded game designer focused on player decisions, feedback, and flow.

A little about my work

I’m Brandon Smith, a game designer pursuing an MFA in Integrated Media Arts at Hunter College. I’m most interested in systems, gameplay, and technical design. I like figuring out how a mechanic should work, building it, testing it, and improving it based on how people actually play.

I start with the player experience. I think about what I want the player to understand or do, what rules create that experience, and what needs to change when something isn’t working. Across more than 20 projects, I’ve worked on gameplay systems, enemy AI, procedural mechanics, editor tools, and other features in Unity, Unreal Engine, and Godot. My goal is to work as a Technical Designer, using both design and implementation to take ideas from a concept to something the team can play, test, and improve.

I work at EGD Collective as an Award Coordinator and Advisor, where I support emerging developers through mentorship, events, project guidance, and industry opportunities. I’ve also worked with Glik Games on PSYCORP and Glikit, a collection of Unity tools built to help designers create and manage content.

Outside of development, I enjoy experimenting with new game ideas, cooking for friends, listening to house music, and playing Warframe.
